Installation and Registration Steps

Download and install the APK first, then open the app to register an account. More detailed instructions can be added later.

1

Open the Download Page

Open your browser and visit the AutoDishes download page.

2

Tap Download or Scroll to the Bottom

Tap the download button directly, or scroll to the bottom of the page to find the download link.

3

Confirm the Download

When your browser displays the download prompt, tap Confirm to download the APK installation file.

4

Open the Downloaded File

Once the download is complete, tap Open to launch the installation file and begin installing.

5

Confirm Installation

When your phone prompts you to install the app, tap Install or Confirm Installation.

6

Open the Installed App

Once installation is complete, tap Open to launch AutoDishes.

7

Tap Register After Opening

For first-time use, tap Register on the login screen to open the account creation page.

8

Enter Your Details and Verification Code

Enter your username, password, phone number, and other details, then follow the prompts to request and enter a verification code.

  • Your username may contain only English letters and numbers.
  • Your password must contain at least 8 characters.
  • Your password must contain at least one number.
  • Your password must contain at least one uppercase English letter.
  • Your password must contain at least one lowercase English letter.
9

Log In After Registering

After registering successfully, return to the login screen and sign in with the account details you just entered.

Installation Tips

The wording of prompts may vary between phone brands, but the overall process is similar.

  • If your browser warns about an app from an unknown source, follow the prompts on your phone to allow this installation.
  • If the installation screen does not open automatically after downloading, find the APK in your browser downloads or file manager.
  • Make sure your phone number is correct when registering, and use the verification code before it expires.
  • If you are not logged in automatically after registering, return to the login screen and enter your new account and password.
